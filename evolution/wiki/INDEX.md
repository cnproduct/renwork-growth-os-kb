# Persistent pattern Wiki

This public Wiki contains only baseline-policy seed patterns. Real pattern evolution belongs in a private, tenant-scoped state directory and should be promoted to shared knowledge only after authorization, de-identification, review, and evaluation.

## Seed patterns

- `PAT-BASE-001`: keep source, claim state, sensitivity, freshness, and publication approval separate.
- `PAT-BASE-002`: preserve tenant isolation and independent buyer evidence/contact/priority grades.
- `PAT-BASE-003`: separate generation, validation, publication, destination verification, and active release.
- `PAT-BASE-004`: separate product signals, diagnosis, browser execution, approval, and measured outcome.

## Pattern rules

- A pattern records evidence and counterexamples, not only a preferred instruction.
- Trace-derived patterns cite redacted trace IDs; baseline policies explicitly identify their non-trace origin.
- Revisions are versioned. Superseded patterns remain discoverable through history.
- Rejected Skill proposals are recorded in `skill-impact.jsonl` in the private state Wiki.
- Privacy deletion and retention enforcement may remove underlying raw data; the Wiki then retains only a lawful tombstone or de-identified lesson.
